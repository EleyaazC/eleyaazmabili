Our Story

Flame & Sea opened its doors in 2021, born out of a love for fire-cooked meats and ocean-fresh seafood. From day one, we've served our community with flavor, passion, and unforgettable hospitality.

Customers have loved our cozy atmosphere, exceptional service, and menus crafted with care. We’re proud to be a place where families, friends, and food lovers gather.
